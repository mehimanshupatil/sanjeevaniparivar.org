# Sanjeevani Parivar

A public trust in Vasai taluka, Maharashtra, doing social work across four kshetras. This glossary fixes the words the site and its docs use for the organisation and its work.

## Organisation

**Public Trust** (सार्वजनिक विश्वस्तव्यवस्था / न्यास):
The organisation's legal form: registered under the Bombay Public Trusts Act, 1950, with the Charity Commissioner's Palghar Division office.
_Avoid_: Society, NGO (in user-facing text), company

**Public Name**:
The name the organisation goes by everywhere except legal contexts: संजीवनी परिवार / Sanjeevani Parivar.
_Avoid_: Sanjeevani Pariwar, Sanjivani Parivar, संजिवनी परिवार

**Legal Name**:
The name exactly as on the trust's registration certificate: संजिवनी परिवार संस्था, नालासोपारा (प). Used only where the law or a bank needs it, such as donation receipts and registration details.
_Avoid_: using it as the display name

**Founding** (स्थापना):
When the organisation began its work: 1994. It is earlier than, and separate from, Registration.
_Avoid_: treating the registration year as the founding year

**Registration** (नोंदणी):
The trust's entry in the public trust register (2008, Palghar Division). Shown only with the Legal Name and registration number.
_Avoid_: Founding, establishment

**Service Area**:
The place the organisation serves, named "Vasai" (वसई) in public text. The registered address in Nalasopara (W), within Vasai taluka, appears only with the Legal Name.
_Avoid_: Nalasopara as the public location

## Publicity

**Publicity** (प्रसिद्धी):
Everything rendered to announce an Edition: its Invitations and Banners.
_Avoid_: Banners (as the name for all of it), creatives, artwork

**Programme**:
A recurring community event the organisation runs, described once: its title, organisers, partner logos and standard wording (e.g. सामवेदी ब्राह्मण ग्राहक पेठ).
_Avoid_: Event, template

**Programme Type**:
The kind of Programme, such as a fair, lecture series, competition or camp. Every Programme of a type, in every year, uses the same Template.
_Avoid_: Category, kshetra (a kshetra is the area of work, not the kind of event)

**Edition**:
One year's occurrence of a Programme: its dates, venue and any wording that differs that year.
_Avoid_: Event, instance, version

**Session** (पुष्प):
One lecture within an Edition of a lecture series, on its own day, with its own speaker and topic (e.g. पुष्प पहिले of संजीवनी व्याख्यानमाला २०२६). It gets its own Invitation besides the Edition's.
_Avoid_: Lecture (for the record), episode, day

**Format**:
One output shape of an Edition's Publicity, such as the WhatsApp card, the Instagram post or a printed flex banner. Each Format is either an Invitation or a Banner.
_Avoid_: Template, size

**Invitation** (निमंत्रण):
Publicity sent to people to invite them, such as the WhatsApp card or the Instagram post.
_Avoid_: Banner, poster

**Banner** (बॅनर):
A printed flex hung at or near the venue to announce the Edition. Only the physical print, never the whole Publicity.
_Avoid_: Invitation, poster, hoarding

**Template**:
The design every Programme of one Programme Type uses, with one layout per Format.
_Avoid_: Programme, Format, theme

**Theme**:
The colours and decorations a Programme's Publicity uses, such as Diwali.
_Avoid_: Skin, style
